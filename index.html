(01) About

I'm picky about what I build. I like owning the whole thing — the UI people touch, the AI that does the work, and the plumbing nobody sees until it breaks at 3am.

Right now I'm at Salesforce Marketing Cloud, on the services behind Agentforce Content Agent. I own cross-cloud auth between Salesforce Core and Studio Services, the provisioning pipeline, and the identity-header architecture the team builds on.

Before that I led backend for enterprise travel & expense platforms at Dice (Cleartrip, IDFC, MakeMyTrip) and did Elasticsearch & Stripe work at Sprinklr. Java (Spring, Vert.x), Node and Python, all on AWS. B.Tech in Computer Engineering from Delhi Technological University (9.1 CGPA), after a JEE rank of 5,649.
